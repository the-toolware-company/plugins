# Toolware support

For installation and connection guidance, start with the
[quick start](README.md#install) and
[connection troubleshooting](system/skills/using-toolware/references/setup.md).

## Contact support

Email [support@thetoolware.company](mailto:support@thetoolware.company).
This is our monitored support mailbox. Include only the information needed
to explain your request; never send passwords, tokens or connection secrets.

## General questions and reproducible bugs

Open an issue at <https://github.com/the-toolware-company/plugins/issues>.
Include your client name/version, plugin version, a short description, and
redacted steps showing the problem. Describe the outcome you expected.

Issues are public. Never include passwords, OAuth tokens, API keys, cookies,
connection secrets, customer records or private workspace content. Redact
screenshots and logs before posting. Do not use public issues for private
account access, privacy requests or vulnerability details.

## Private requests

For private account or service help, email
[support@thetoolware.company](mailto:support@thetoolware.company) instead of
opening a public issue. The dedicated privacy contact, if different, will be
specified in the final privacy notice.

Support hours and response targets have not been committed in this package.
The GitHub issue route is not an emergency channel.
